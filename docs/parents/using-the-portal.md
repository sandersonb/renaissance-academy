# Using the portal

After you log in, your **dashboard** lists your students, their current status, and
any actions you can take.

## Add a student (new applicant)

1. Select **Add a student**.
2. Enter the student's name, date of birth, gender, and the grade they'd enter.
3. Submit. The student joins the **lottery pool** with the status *Awaiting Lottery*.

Kindergarten has an age cutoff; the form will let you know if a birth date doesn't
qualify.

## Re-enroll a current student

If your student **currently attends** Renaissance Academy, add them the same way and
indicate they're a returning/current student. The system matches them to the school
roster by **name and date of birth**, so enter those exactly as the school has them.
If the match succeeds, they're re-enrolled for next year (their grade comes from the
school's records). If it can't find a match, double-check spelling and the birth
date, or contact the enrollment office.

## Statuses you might see

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting Lottery** | In the pool; the draw hasn't happened yet. |
| **Offer Extended** | A position is available — action needed before it expires. |
| **Waitlisted / Sibling Waitlisted** | Not selected yet; you'll see your position and may be offered a spot if one opens. |
| **Awaiting Paperwork** | You accepted — now complete and return the registration forms by the deadline. |
| **Registered** | All done — your student is enrolled. |
| **Accepted Re-Enrollment** | A returning student's spot is confirmed for next year. |

## Accept or decline an offer

When a student has an **offer**, you'll see **Accept** and **Decline** buttons.

- **Accept** — you may be asked a few registration questions (for example,
  kindergarten session or language preference). After accepting, your student moves
  to **Awaiting Paperwork** and you'll download the packets (below).
- **Decline** — releases the position. This can't be undone, so only decline if
  you're sure.

**Watch the deadline.** Offers expire on the date shown. An expired offer may be
released to the next family on the waiting list.

## The waiting list

If your student is waitlisted, the dashboard shows their **position**. If a seat
opens, the next student on the list is offered it and you'll be emailed. Students
with a sibling already attending are offered ahead of the regular waiting list.

## Download your registration paperwork

Once you've accepted (or for returning families, when it's time), a **Registration
paperwork** section appears on your dashboard with the packet(s) you need — your
grade's packet and the appropriate family packet.

1. Download and print each packet.
2. Complete them.
3. **Email or bring the completed forms to the enrollment specialist.** Do **not**
   upload them through this site — for privacy, completed paperwork is handled
   directly with the office.

If a link says a packet isn't available yet, the office is still preparing that
year's forms — check back shortly.

## Signing out

Use **Log out** when you're done, especially on a shared computer.

## Need help?

For anything the site can't resolve — a returning student who won't match, a missed
deadline, or a question about paperwork — contact the enrollment office.
