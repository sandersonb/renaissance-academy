# Getting started

## Create your account

The account is for **you, the parent or guardian** — not for a student. You'll add
your student(s) after signing in.

1. Go to **`https://enrollment.renacademy.org`** and choose **Register / Create a
   parent account**.
2. Fill in your name, contact information, and a username and password.
3. Submit the form.

**One account per family.** If both parents try to register separately you'll end up
with two families — instead, share one login.

## Confirm your email

After registering, you'll get a **confirmation email**. Open it and select the link
to activate your account. (You may be asked a couple of short questions as part of
confirming.) You can't log in until your email is confirmed.

Didn't get it? Check spam, then use the **resend confirmation** option on the site.

## Log in

Once confirmed, go to the site and **Log in** with your username and password. If
your username and password don't match, the site will simply say the combination
isn't valid — for your security it won't reveal whether a username exists.

## Forgot your password?

Choose **Forgot password**, enter your username and email, and you'll receive a
reset link if they match an account. Follow the link to set a new password, then log
in. (For your privacy, the site won't tell you whether an account exists — you'll
just get the email if it does.)
