# Parent Guidebook

Welcome! This guide explains how to use the Renaissance Academy enrollment site to
apply for a spot, accept an offer, and complete registration.

The site is at **`https://enrollment.renacademy.org`**.

## Guides

1. **[Getting started](getting-started.md)** — creating your account, confirming your
   email, logging in, and resetting a forgotten password.
2. **[Using the portal](using-the-portal.md)** — adding a student, re-enrolling a
   current student, accepting or declining an offer, the waiting list, and
   downloading your registration paperwork.

## How enrollment works, briefly

- You create **one parent/guardian account** and add your student(s) to it.
- New applicants enter a **lottery**. After the draw, some receive an **offer** and
  others join a **waiting list**.
- If your student receives an offer, you **accept** it in the portal, then complete
  and return the **registration paperwork**.
- **Current students** re-enroll each year through the same site.

You'll get **emails** at important moments (confirm your account, a position is
available, reminders about deadlines). Please keep an eye on your inbox — offers and
paperwork have deadlines.
